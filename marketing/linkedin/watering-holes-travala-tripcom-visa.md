# Lead Sourcing from Travala / Trip.com / Visa Ecosystems

**Key reframe:** None of these three are leads themselves.
- **Travala** already runs FurlPay's exact stack (Travel MCP on x402 + gasless USDC on Base, ~$0.01/booking) → a **competitor/reference-architecture**, not a buyer.
- **Trip.com** is a giant OTA → too big to sell to cold; useful for its **partner/affiliate/supplier network**.
- **Visa** is a rail owner running its own USDC settlement (9 chains, $7B run-rate) → a **competitor/standard-setter**, not a buyer.

The value is their **public ecosystems** (partners, merchants, suppliers) and their **publicly-active LinkedIn audiences**. All tactics below use public pages + normal LinkedIn engagement. No scraping, no follower/employee harvesting.

---

## Travala — mine the crypto-travel ecosystem
- **What's public:** partner list (Crypto.com Pay, aggregators feeding 2.2M+ hotel listings — Marriott/Hilton/IHG via aggregators), event sponsors, and their July 2026 crypto+travel events calendar.
- **Leads hiding here:**
  - **Smaller OTAs / booking platforms** that want crypto checkout but *don't* have Travala's in-house x402 build → Segment B (Connect) + Segment A (x402 checkout).
  - **Aggregators & travel-tech vendors** in their supply chain who move cross-border money to hotels.
- **Tactic:** attend/monitor their public crypto-travel events (virtual). Speakers and sponsor companies are named publicly → research those companies' finance/eng leaders on LinkedIn → normal outreach with the travel-crypto-settlement angle.

## Trip.com — mine the partner & affiliate programs
- **What's public:** the Affiliate Program (`trip.com/partners`), the B2B API portal (`connect.trip.com`), and `group.trip.com/partners`.
- **Leads hiding here:**
  - **Affiliates & sub-agencies** getting paid commission cross-border (40–60 day settlement, $200 threshold) — they feel FX + payout pain acutely → Segment B.
  - **Companies building on the Trip.com API** (travel-tech startups) → Segment A/B.
- **Tactic:** the affiliate/partner directories name companies publicly. Build a shortlist of the mid-market ones, find their finance lead on LinkedIn, lead with "cutting cross-border payout fees + settlement time."

## Visa — mine the stablecoin-settlement partner announcements
- **What's public:** Visa's own press releases name every settlement partner: **Cross River Bank, Lead Bank**, and chain partners **Arc (Circle), Base (Coinbase), Canton, Polygon, Tempo**.
- **Leads hiding here:** the **fintechs, issuers, and acquirers** that plug into Visa's stablecoin pilot but still need cheaper *merchant-side* settlement or *agentic* payments Visa doesn't offer.
- **Tactic:** Visa validates the whole thesis (stablecoin settlement is mainstream — $7B run-rate). Use that as **social proof in your content** ("Visa now settles on 9 chains — here's what that means for mid-market platforms Visa won't serve directly"). Then the companies commenting on / reacting to Visa's stablecoin posts are self-identified interested prospects → engage them.

---

## The one legitimate "extraction" method: engage the public audience
You cannot export their followers. You **can**:
1. Watch the **comment sections** of Travala/Visa/x402 posts — people who comment are publicly signaling interest and are fair game to engage and connect with.
2. Follow the **hashtags** these companies use (`#x402`, `#stablecoins`, `#agenticpayments`) and engage original posters.
3. Use **LinkedIn Premium search** to find people at the *named partner companies* above (Cross River, Lead Bank, affiliates, ecosystem vendors) — that's Premium's intended use, not scraping.
