# Day-1 Profile Optimization

Decision-makers click your profile before they reply. Make it credible in 5 seconds.

## Founder personal profile

- **Headline:** `Founder @ FurlPay | Cutting B2B payment fees to 0.5% + secure x402 payments for AI agents (USDC on Base/Solana)`
- **Banner:** clean graphic — "The stablecoin settlement + agentic-payment rail." Keep it text-light.
- **About** (3 short paragraphs):
  1. **Problem** — B2B platforms bleed 2.5–4% on card interchange + SWIFT; AI agents can't pay per-call on card rails at all.
  2. **Solution** — FurlPay: flat 0.5% stablecoin settlement (Furlpay Connect) + hardened x402 pay-per-call API for machine payments.
  3. **CTA** — "Running a closed pilot for mid-market platforms. DM me or book: [link]."
- **Creator mode:** ON. Content pillars: `Stablecoins`, `Agentic Payments`, `x402`, `Account Abstraction`.
- **Featured section:** pin the x402 security post → `https://furlpay.com/blog/five-ways-to-rob-an-ai-agent-securing-x402`.

## Company page (linkedin.com/company/furlpay)

- Tagline: "The trust layer for stablecoin settlement & machine payments."
- Fill every field (logo, banner, website, industry, specialties: `stablecoins, x402, USDC, cross-border settlement, agentic payments`). Complete pages get materially more reach.
- Add 1–2 team members as page admins so posts can be reshared to personal networks day one.
- Pin the Connect launch post.

## The "Power of Two" split

- **Company page** = official archive (product releases, docs launches, milestones).
- **Founder account** = story-driven, human voice, 3x/week. Founder posts routinely out-reach company pages 5–10x. Company page reshares founder posts, not the reverse.
