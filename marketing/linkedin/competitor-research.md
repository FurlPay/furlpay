All methods here are sanctioned — public tools, native LinkedIn features, and the authenticated LinkedIn MCP in this session. No scraping.

## The 4 monitoring channels

### A. LinkedIn Ad Library (public, paid-strategy intel)
- `linkedin.com/ad-library` → search competitor → filter Active Ads + Region.
- Look for: lead magnets (e.g. Circle's "State of the Stablecoin" report), onboarding hooks, video vs. static.

### B. Native Page Analytics → Competitors (once you admin the FurlPay page)
- Page admin → Analytics → Competitors → Add: Stripe, Coinbase, Circle, Travala.
- Live scoreboard: posting frequency, new followers, engagement rate.

### C. Executive "amplifier" accounts (where the real reach is)
- Stripe: Patrick Collison, John Collison · Coinbase: Brian Armstrong · Circle: Jeremy Allaire.
- Watch their post templates, which threads they comment on, how they announce launches. These are also your Week-2 strategic-commenting targets.

### D. Social listening
- Track `#x402`, `#stablecoins`, `#fintech`, `#cryptotravel` (Brand24 / BuzzSumo / Mention, or manual saved searches).
